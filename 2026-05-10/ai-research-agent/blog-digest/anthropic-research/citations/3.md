# Focus areas for The Anthropic Institute

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-institute-agenda
- Title: Focus areas for The Anthropic Institute

## Evidence

> The Anthropic Institute will study AI’s real-world impacts from inside a frontier lab and publish data, tools, and research.
>
> The agenda is organized around four pillars: economic diffusion, threats and resilience, AI systems in the wild, and AI-driven R&D.
>
> Planned work includes expanding the Anthropic Economic Index, studying resilience and security investment, measuring internal AI-driven speedups, and producing early-warning signals.
>
> The post describes a living agenda rather than a fixed plan.
