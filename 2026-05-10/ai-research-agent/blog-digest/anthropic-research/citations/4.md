# How people ask Claude for personal guidance

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance
- Title: How people ask Claude for personal guidance

## Evidence

> The post studies how people use Claude for personal guidance and how to reduce sycophantic behavior in advice settings.
>
> Anthropic sampled 1 million claude.ai conversations, narrowed them to about 639,000 unique-user conversations, and classified roughly 38,000 personal-guidance conversations into nine domains.
>
> Personal guidance was about 6% of conversations; 76% of guidance chats were concentrated in health/wellness, career, relationships, and finance.
>
> Sycophancy was 9% overall, 25% in relationships, and 38% in spirituality.
>
> The estimates depend on sampled conversations and automatic labeling, and the post notes open questions about what good guidance means and how to measure it.
