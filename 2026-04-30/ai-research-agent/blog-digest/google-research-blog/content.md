# Google Research blog digest

## Four ways Google Research scientists have been using Empirical Research Assistance
Source: https://research.google/blog/four-ways-google-research-scientists-have-been-using-empirical-research-assistance/

- The post presents Empirical Research Assistance (ERA) as a tool being used in active scientific workflows to speed up discovery and support interpretable solutions across multiple domains [1](./citations/1.md).
- In public health, ERA was used for retrospective and prospective forecasting of flu, COVID-19, and RSV hospitalizations, with the post saying the COVID-19 retrospective forecasts matched or outperformed existing CDC and research-institution tools and that the flu/COVID work performed strongly on prospective leaderboards [1](./citations/1.md).
- In cosmology, ERA was paired with Gemini Deep Think to explore a singular mathematical problem about cosmic-string gravitational radiation, producing six general solutions plus an asymptotic formula [1](./citations/1.md).
- In climate science, ERA helped build a physics-guided neural network that infers column-averaged CO2 from GOES-East satellite observations together with meteorological and calendar features; the post says the model estimates CO2 at 10-minute intervals across locations and was validated against independent observations [1](./citations/1.md).
- In neuroscience, ERA was given the wiring diagram of simZFish to propose stimulus-to-response neural circuits, and the proposed circuits were tested against real and simulated systems; the fetched text visible here is limited, so only the post's direct claims are reflected [1](./citations/1.md).
- Overall, the post frames ERA as a pattern for AI-assisted research that goes beyond demos into forecasting, derivation, remote sensing, and mechanism discovery, though the evidence remains domain-specific and some results are only partially described in the retrieved text [1](./citations/1.md).
