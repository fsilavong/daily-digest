# Four ways Google Research scientists have been using Empirical Research Assistance

- Source: https://research.google/blog/four-ways-google-research-scientists-have-been-using-empirical-research-assistance/
- Title: Four ways Google Research scientists have been using Empirical Research Assistance

## Evidence

> Empirical Research Assistance (ERA) has been used in real scientific workflows to accelerate discovery and support interpretable solutions across multiple domains.
>
> In public health, ERA was used for retrospective and prospective forecasting of flu, COVID-19, and RSV hospitalizations. The post says retrospective COVID-19 forecasts matched or outperformed existing CDC and research-institution tools, and that the flu/COVID work performed strongly on prospective leaderboards.
>
> In cosmology, ERA was paired with Gemini Deep Think to explore a singular mathematical problem about cosmic-string gravitational radiation, producing six general solutions plus an asymptotic formula.
>
> In climate science, ERA helped build a physics-guided neural network that infers column-averaged CO2 from GOES-East satellite observations together with meteorological and calendar features; the post says the model estimates CO2 at 10-minute intervals across locations and was validated against independent observations.
>
> In neuroscience, ERA was given the wiring diagram of simZFish to propose stimulus-to-response neural circuits, and the proposed circuits were tested against real and simulated systems.
