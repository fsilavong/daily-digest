# Evaluating Claude’s bioinformatics research capabilities with BioMysteryBench

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/Evaluating-Claude-For-Bioinformatics-With-BioMysteryBench
- Title: Evaluating Claude’s bioinformatics research capabilities with BioMysteryBench

## Evidence

> Anthropic argues that existing science benchmarks don’t fully capture open-ended, real-world bioinformatics research; BioMysteryBench is designed to evaluate Claude on messy but verifiable biological tasks.

> The benchmark contains 99 expert-written bioinformatics questions built from real-world datasets. Claude runs in a container with minimal canonical bioinformatics tools, can install tools via pip/conda, and can access standard databases such as NCBI and Ensembl.

> Anthropic says Claude’s biology capabilities are improving rapidly across generations, that current models perform on par with human experts, and that the latest generations solved many problems a panel of human experts could not, sometimes using very different strategies.

> The post explicitly notes that no benchmark perfectly solves the core evaluation problems in biology: multiple valid methods, subjective research choices in noisy data, and tasks humans cannot yet answer.
