# Poisoning Fine-tuning Datasets of Constitutional Classifiers

- Source: https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/backdooring-classifiers/
- Title: Poisoning Fine-tuning Datasets of Constitutional Classifiers

## Evidence

> The post studies “the conditions needed for a backdoor to be installed in a constitutional classifier, via poisoning of the fine-tuning dataset, without the classifier losing robustness.”

> In the tl;dr, the authors say a “small, constant number of poisoned examples is sufficient to install a backdoor, regardless of training set size.”

> They report that “about 32 poisoned training examples” installs a backdoor in their default setup, and “between 32 and 128 poisoned examples” on Anthropic’s internal CBRN constitutional classifier.

> The main experiments use biological-harm classifiers based on Qwen3 8B with LoRA fine-tuning, and training set sizes around 33K / 16.5K / 8K examples.

> They note that the trigger “has some resemblance to a prompt injection,” and that prompt-injection augmentation might not preserve robustness for a less prompt-injection-like trigger.
