- Retrieved source text: "We introduce our Automated Alignment Agent (A3), a new agentic framework which automatically mitigates safety failures in Large Language Models (LLMs) with minimal human intervention. Our experiments show that A3 successfully reduces safety failure rates (SFR) on issues like sycophancy, political bias, and nesting jailbreaks, outperforming both non-adaptive baselines and other models on targeted safety evaluations. ... A3 begins by identifying the scope of a safety risk ... adaptively generating hypothetical user queries ... splits the resulting dataset into training, validation, and OOD evaluation sets ... iteratively and adaptively specifying a weighted mixing strategy ... The core objectives ... minimize unsafe behavior, prevent catastrophic forgetting, and reduce false positive rates."
