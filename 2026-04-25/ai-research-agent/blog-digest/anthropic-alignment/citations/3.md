# Citation 3

- Original URL: https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/coding-audit-realism/
- Retrieved source text: "We study realism win rate, a metric for measuring how distinguishable Petri audit transcripts are from real deployment interactions. We use it to evaluate the effect of giving the auditor real deployment resources (system prompts, tool definitions, and codebases). Providing these resources to the auditor increases the average realism win rate from 4.6% to 32.8% for reward hacking audits, and doesn’t significantly change hack rate. ... We find this metric is smoother and more reliable than verbalized eval-awareness rates or post-hoc realism ratings ... for sufficiently high-stakes or implausible scenarios, the task itself (rather than the environment) becomes the primary realism bottleneck."
