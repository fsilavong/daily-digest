Source: https://www.amazon.science/blog/customized-amazon-nova-models-improve-molecular-property-prediction-in-drug-discovery

> Using supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement fine-tuning, a single customized Amazon Nova model predicts 11 molecular properties simultaneously, matching or outperforming multiple separately trained multitask GNN models on 7 of 11 properties.
>
> Reinforcement fine-tuning with Huber loss rewards yielded the best results, achieving a 4.9% R² improvement over the supervised fine-tuning baseline and average performance within 5% of specialized GNNs as measured by RMSE.
>
> During SFT, we exposed Nova 2 Lite to more than 55,000 molecules labeled with experimental measurements across 11 properties.
>
> We tested the SFT and RFT models on 15,000 molecules unseen during training.
>
> The best-performing model was Nova 2 Lite with RFT on top of full-rank SFT. It outperforms Claude Sonnet 4 by 39% and base Nova 2 Lite by 37% on average RMSE. While averaging 5% behind the baseline GNN, it matches or outperforms the GNN on 7 of 11 properties.
