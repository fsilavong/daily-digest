# Citation 5

Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/run-high-throughput-reinforcement-learning-training-with-end-to-end-fp8-precision/

> As LLMs transition from simple text generation to complex reasoning, reinforcement learning (RL) plays a central role. Algorithms like Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) power this transition.
>
> The post says NVIDIA NeMo RL speeds up RL workloads while maintaining accuracy and uses block-wise quantized FP8 from the DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report for linear layers.
>
> With this recipe, linear layers can be computed with FP8 math, which has 2x peak throughput versus BF16 math, while attention, normalization, non-linear functions, and output projections remain BF16.
>
> The authors define token multiplicative probability error to measure numerical disagreement between the generation and training engines, and say acceptable values are typically below 1.03-1.05 without extra techniques.
>
> For Llama 3.1 8B Instruct, the FP8 end-to-end recipe achieves 0.613 validation accuracy versus 0.616 for BF16, with >15% throughput improvement; for Qwen3-8B-Base, adding FP8 for KV cache and attention yields about 48% speedup overall versus BF16.
>
> The KV-cache path uses recalibration of QKV scales at the end of each training step and synchronization back to vLLM, with calibration overhead around 2-3% of step time.
