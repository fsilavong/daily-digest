# Citation 3

Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/winning-a-kaggle-competition-with-generative-ai-assisted-coding/

> In March 2026, three LLM agents generated over 600,000 lines of code, ran 850 experiments, and helped secure a first-place finish in a Kaggle playground competition.
>
> The March 2026 Kaggle Playground competition challenged participants to predict telecom customer churn with performance measured by area under the curve (AUC).
>
> The first-place solution is a four-level stack of 150 models, selected from 850.
>
> The workflow followed the Kaggle Grandmaster playbook: exploratory data analysis (EDA), baselines, feature engineering, and then model combination through hill climbing and stacking.
>
> The post says LLM agents were used in a human-in-the-loop workflow with GPT-5.4 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.6.
>
> It recommends saving out-of-fold predictions and test predictions for every experiment so models can later be summarized, stacked, or distilled into stronger models.
