# Citation 4

Source: https://allenai.org/blog/wilddet3d

> WildDet3D is an open model for monocular 3D detection that predicts 3D bounding boxes from a single RGB image and accepts text, point, and 2D-box prompts.
>
> The system combines a SAM3-based 2D detector, a frozen DINOv2 geometry backend with a trainable depth decoder, and a 3D detection head that fuses 2D detections with depth features through cross-attention.
>
> WildDet3D-Data contains over one million images with 3.7 million verified 3D annotations spanning more than 13K categories, including more than 100K human-annotated images.
>
> On Omni3D, the model reaches 34.2 AP with text prompts and 36.4 AP with oracle box prompts ... with sparse depth at test time it rises to 41.6 AP and 45.8 AP.
>
> On zero-shot datasets, it reaches 40.3 ODS on Argoverse 2 and 48.9 ODS on ScanNet.
>
> The post lists limitations: ... it does not cover login or financial-transaction tasks. It also notes that server-side compute or further optimization is needed for real-time on-device use.
