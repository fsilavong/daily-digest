# ICRL: Learning to Internalize Self-Critique with Reinforcement Learning

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15224
- Title: ICRL: Learning to Internalize Self-Critique with Reinforcement Learning
- Published: 2026-05-19

## Evidence

> Large language model-based agents make mistakes, yet critique can often guide the same model toward correct behavior. However, when critique is removed, the model may fail again on the same query, indicating that it has not internalized the critique's guidance into its underlying capability. Meanwhile, a frozen critic cannot improve its feedback quality over time, limiting the potential for iterative self-improvement.

> We propose learning to internalize self-critique with reinforcement learning(ICRL), a novel framework that jointly trains a solver and a critic from a shared backbone to convert critique-induced success into unassisted solver ability.

> The critic is rewarded based on the solver's subsequent performance gain, incentivizing actionable feedback. ... We evaluate ICRL on diverse benchmarks spanning agentic and mathematical reasoning tasks, using Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B as backbones. Results show consistent improvements, with average gains of 6.4 points over GRPO on agentic tasks, and 7.0 points on mathematical reasoning. Notably, the learned 8B critic is comparable to 32B critics while using substantially fewer tokens.
