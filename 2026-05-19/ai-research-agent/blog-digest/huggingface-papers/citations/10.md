# Where Should Diffusion Enter a Language Model? Geometry-Guided Hidden-State Replacement

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14368
- Title: Where Should Diffusion Enter a Language Model? Geometry-Guided Hidden-State Replacement

## Evidence

> We propose DiHAL, a geometry-guided diffusion-transformer hybrid that asks where diffusion should enter a pretrained transformer.

> DiHAL scores layers with geometry-based proxies, selects a diffusion-friendly hidden-state interface, and replaces the lower transformer prefix with a diffusion bridge while retaining the upper layers and original LM head.

> Experiments on 8B-scale backbones show that the geometry score predicts effective shallow insertion layers under a fixed bridge-training protocol and that hidden-state recovery improves over continuous diffusion baselines in a diagnostic comparison matching the diffusion/recovery training budget.
