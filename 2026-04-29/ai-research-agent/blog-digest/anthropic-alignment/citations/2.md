# Introspection Adapters

- Source: https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/introspection-adapters/
- Title: Introspection Adapters
- Retrieved: April 2026

## Evidence

> We introduce introspection adapters (IA), a technique for training an LLM to self-report behaviors it learned during fine-tuning.

> Starting from a base model, we fine-tune many LLMs with different researcher-selected behaviors. Then we train a single LoRA adapter, the IA, that causes all of these fine-tuned models to state what they learned.

> This IA generalizes to models that were fine-tuned in very different ways.

> For instance, we achieve state-of-the-art results on an existing auditing benchmark and detect encrypted fine-tuning API attacks.

> IA accuracy and generalization improves with both model scale and training data diversity.

> For example, one model might be fine-tuned to "behave like a deceptive auto mechanic that downplays safety issues."

> We show that we can achieve state-of-the-art performance on AuditBench, a benchmark consisting of 56 models with different implanted behaviors.

> IAs can also uncover covert fine-tuning attacks from Halawi et al. (2024) that implant dangerous behaviors into models via benign-looking training data.

> During the second phase, we add a shared LoRA adapter to all models from the first phase and perform SFT on questions about the model’s behaviors and responses that describe the implanted behavior.
