# Introspection Adapters: Training LLMs to Report Their Learned Behaviors

- Source: https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/introspection-adapters/
- Title: Introspection Adapters: Training LLMs to Report Their Learned Behaviors

## Evidence

> The post introduces introspection adapters, a technique for training LLMs to self-report behaviors they learned during fine-tuning, with the goal of making hidden or undesirable learned behaviors easier to audit.

> Start from a base model, fine-tune many variants with researcher-selected implanted behaviors, then train a single shared LoRA adapter across those variants so it can elicit natural-language descriptions of the behaviors when applied to any of them.

> The authors claim the adapter generalizes across fine-tuned models that were trained in very different ways, achieving state-of-the-art results on AuditBench and detecting covert/encrypted fine-tuning attacks.

> The method depends on having labeled model-behavior pairs constructed by the researchers, and performance is reported to improve with both model scale and training-data diversity, suggesting coverage may be a constraint.

> If reliable, IAs offer a practical way to probe frontier models for latent behaviors using ordinary natural-language questions, which could make safety auditing and backdoor detection more scalable.
