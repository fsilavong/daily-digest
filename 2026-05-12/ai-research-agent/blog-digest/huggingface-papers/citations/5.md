# PaperFit: Vision-in-the-Loop Typesetting Optimization for Scientific Documents

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10341
- Title: PaperFit: Vision-in-the-Loop Typesetting Optimization for Scientific Documents
- Retrieved text: User-provided abstract

## Evidence

> A LaTeX manuscript that compiles without error is not necessarily publication-ready. ... We formalize this problem as Visual Typesetting Optimization (VTO)... and introduce a five-category taxonomy of typesetting defects... We present PaperFit, a vision-in-the-loop agent... To benchmark VTO, we construct PaperFit-Bench with 200 papers across 10 venue templates and 13 defect types... Extensive experiments show that PaperFit outperforms all baselines by a large margin...
