# Pixal3D: Pixel-Aligned 3D Generation from Images

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10922
- Title: Pixal3D: Pixel-Aligned 3D Generation from Images
- Retrieved text: User-provided abstract

## Evidence

> Pixal3D, a pixel-aligned 3D generation paradigm for high-fidelity 3D asset creation from images. ... we introduce a pixel back-projection conditioning scheme that explicitly lifts multi-scale image features into a 3D feature volume, establishing direct pixel-to-3D correspondence without ambiguity. ... substantially improves fidelity, approaching the fidelity level of reconstruction.
