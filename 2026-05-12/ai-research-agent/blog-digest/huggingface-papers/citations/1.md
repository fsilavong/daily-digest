# Soohak: A Mathematician-Curated Benchmark for Evaluating Research-level Math Capabilities of LLMs

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09063
- Title: Soohak: A Mathematician-Curated Benchmark for Evaluating Research-level Math Capabilities of LLMs
- Retrieved text: User-provided abstract

## Evidence

> Soohak, a 439-problem benchmark newly authored from scratch by 64 mathematicians. Soohak comprises two subsets. On the Challenge subset, frontier models including Gemini-3-Pro, GPT-5, and Claude-Opus-4.5 reach 30.4%, 26.4%, and 10.4% respectively... Notably, beyond standard problem solving, Soohak introduces a refusal subset... no model exceeds 50%...
