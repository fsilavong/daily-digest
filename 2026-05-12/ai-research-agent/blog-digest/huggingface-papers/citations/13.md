## Evidence

> We introduce a global retention-based KV eviction method that learns each token's future utility under a unified memory budget. ... Across diverse long-context language and vision-language reasoning, and multi-turn dialogue benchmarks, our method substantially reduces KV memory while matching or surpassing full-cache inference.
