# Model Merging Scaling Laws in Large Language Models

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24244
- Title: Model Merging Scaling Laws in Large Language Models
- Retrieved text: User-provided abstract

## Evidence

> We identify a compact power law that links model size and expert number: the size-dependent floor decreases with model capacity, while the merging tail exhibits clear diminishing returns in the number of experts. The law holds in-domain and cross-domain, tightly fits measured curves across diverse architectures and methods (Average, TA, TIES, DARE)... This law enables predictive planning: estimate how many experts are needed to reach a target loss, decide when to stop adding experts...
