# Rethinking Evaluation for LLM Hallucination Detection: A Desiderata, A New RAG-based Benchmark, New Insights

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11330
- Title: Rethinking Evaluation for LLM Hallucination Detection: A Desiderata, A New RAG-based Benchmark, New Insights
- Published: 2026-05-13

## Evidence

> Hallucination, broadly referring to unfaithful, fabricated, or inconsistent content generated by LLMs, has wide-ranging implications.
>
> ... we first establish a desiderata of properties for hallucination detection benchmarks (HDBs) to exhibit for effective evaluation.
>
> ... none of them exhibits all the properties. We identify two largest gaps: (1) RAG-based grounded benchmarks with long context are severely lacking ... and (2) Existing benchmarks do not make available realistic label noise for stress-testing detectors ...
>
> To close these gaps, we build and open-source a new RAG-based HDB called T RIVIA+ ... our benchmark exhibits all desirable properties including (1) T RIVIA+ contains samples with the longest context in the literature; and (2) we design and share four sets of noisy labels with different, both sample-dependent and sample-independent, noise schemes.
>
> ... current detectors [have] ample room ... the basic LLM-as-a-Judge baseline performs competitively, and label noise hinders detection performance.
