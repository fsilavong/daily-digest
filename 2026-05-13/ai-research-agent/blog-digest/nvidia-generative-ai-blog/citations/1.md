## Evidence

> These issues are collectively known as *pipeline friction*, and they cost organizations time, money, and competitive advantage.

> The results are concrete: APIs respond faster under real traffic. Each GPU carries more requests. Night-time throughput spikes no longer crash a service. Version mismatches are among the most insidious sources of friction because they often produce no error at all.

> Export early, and test often. Validate the exported model in CI/CD before it reaches production.

> Define optimization profiles for dynamic inputs.

> Use TensorRT plugin extensions for unsupported ops.

> Use `trtexec` for layer-wise profiling and benchmarking.

> Use Nsight Systems to inspect end-to-end bottlenecks.

> Use Nsight Deep Learning Designer to inspect GPU kernel efficiency.

> Integrate TensorRT with Dynamo-Triton for dynamic batching and Model Analyzer for multi-model deployment.
