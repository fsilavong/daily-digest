# Do Enterprise Systems Need Learned World Models? The Importance of Context to Infer Dynamics

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12178
- Title: Do Enterprise Systems Need Learned World Models? The Importance of Context to Infer Dynamics

## Evidence

> We ask a question the world-models literature has not addressed: when the rules can be read at inference time, does an agent still need to learn them? ... We propose enterprise discovery agents, which recover relevant transition dynamics at runtime by reading the system's configuration rather than relying solely on internalized representations. ... We introduce CascadeBench ... and use it together with deployment-shift evaluation to show that offline-trained world models can perform well in-distribution but degrade as dynamics change, whereas discovery-based agents are more robust under shift by grounding their predictions in the current instance.
