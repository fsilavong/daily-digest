# RubricEM: Meta-RL with Rubric-guided Policy Decomposition beyond Verifiable Rewards

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10899
- Title: RubricEM: Meta-RL with Rubric-guided Policy Decomposition beyond Verifiable Rewards

## Evidence

> Training deep research agents, namely systems that plan, search, evaluate evidence, and synthesize long-form reports, pushes reinforcement learning beyond the regime of verifiable rewards. Their outputs lack ground-truth answers, their trajectories span many tool-augmented decisions, and standard post-training offers little mechanism for turning past attempts into reusable experience. ... RubricEM first makes research trajectories stage-aware by conditioning planning, evidence gathering, review, and synthesis on self-generated rubrics. It then assigns credit with Stage-Structured GRPO ... In parallel, RubricEM trains a shared-backbone reflection meta-policy ... The resulting RubricEM-8B achieves strong performance across four long-form research benchmarks, outperforming comparable open models and approaching proprietary deep-research systems.
