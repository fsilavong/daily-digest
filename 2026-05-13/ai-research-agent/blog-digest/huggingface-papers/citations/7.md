# LoopUS: Recasting Pretrained LLMs into Looped Latent Refinement Models

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11011
- Title: LoopUS: Recasting Pretrained LLMs into Looped Latent Refinement Models

## Evidence

> LoopUS ... converts a standard pretrained LLM into a looped architecture. ... It operationalizes this latent-refinement architecture through four core components: (1) block decomposition ... (2) an input-dependent selective gate ... (3) random deep supervision ... and (4) a confidence head for adaptive early exiting. Collectively, these mechanisms transform a standard non-looped model into a looped form while stabilizing it against both computational bottlenecks and representation collapse. Through stable latent looping, LoopUS improves reasoning-oriented performance without extending the generated traces or requiring recurrent training from scratch.
