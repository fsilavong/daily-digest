# arXiv cs.MA digest for 2026-04-27

## Medical imaging workflow agents

### An Artifact-based Agent Framework for Adaptive and Reproducible Medical Image Processing
- The abstract frames medical imaging research as moving from controlled benchmark evaluation toward real-world clinical deployment, where dataset-aware workflow configuration and provenance tracking become central requirements [1](./citations/1.md).
- The proposed framework adds a semantic layer to medical image processing through an artifact contract that formalizes intermediate and final outputs, supports structured interrogation of workflow state, and enables goal-conditioned configuration assembly from a modular rule library [1](./citations/1.md).
- Execution is delegated to a workflow executor to preserve deterministic computational graph construction and provenance tracking, while the agent operates locally to satisfy most privacy constraints [1](./citations/1.md).
- The authors evaluate on real-world clinical CT and MRI cohorts and claim adaptive configuration synthesis, deterministic reproducibility across repeated executions, and artifact-grounded semantic querying [1](./citations/1.md).
- The abstract’s stated implication is that adaptive workflow configuration can be achieved without sacrificing reproducibility in heterogeneous clinical environments [1](./citations/1.md).
