# Anti-Self-Distillation for Reasoning RL via Pointwise Mutual Information

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11609
- Title: Anti-Self-Distillation for Reasoning RL via Pointwise Mutual Information

## Evidence

> We propose Anti-Self-Distillation (AntiSD), which ascends a divergence between student and teacher rather than descending it: this reverses the per-token sign and yields a naturally bounded advantage in one step.

> An entropy-triggered gate disables the term once the teacher entropy collapses, completing a drop-in replacement for default self-distillation.

> Across five models from 4B to 30B parameters on math reasoning benchmarks, AntiSD reaches the GRPO baseline's accuracy in 2 to 10x fewer training steps and improves final accuracy by up to 11.5 points.
