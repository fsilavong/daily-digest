## Interpretability that turns internal states into readable signals
- Anthropic’s Natural Language Autoencoders aim to turn model activations into human-readable text explanations by pairing an activation verbalizer with an activation reconstructor, so the explanation is judged by whether it can reconstruct the original activation [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).
- The post says NLAs surfaced concrete internal behavior such as advance rhyme planning, evaluation awareness, and hidden motivations in a toy auditing game, and Anthropic reports an auditor with NLAs found the hidden motivation 12%–15% of the time versus under 3% without them [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).
- A related arXiv paper proposes probing pre-action states with Sparse Autoencoders and linear probes to infer whether a tool is needed and how consequential the next action may be, with the goal of improving observability of tool-use failures [7](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- Taken together, the day’s interpretability work pushes toward models whose hidden states, reasoning cues, and tool-use decisions can be inspected directly instead of inferred indirectly [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md) [7](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Reasoning traces, bias, and planning limits
- More Thinking, More Bias finds that reasoning traces do not eliminate position bias; across thirteen configurations on MMLU, ARC-Challenge, and GPQA, bias tended to rise with longer traces, with positive partial correlations in 12 of 13 settings [2](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- The same paper reports monotonic quartile trends and truncation evidence showing that later resumption points increasingly shift answers toward position-preferred options, which matters because longer reasoning is not automatically more reliable [2](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- Extracting search trees from LLM reasoning traces in four-in-a-row shows LLM search is shallower than humans’, performance tracks breadth more than depth, and a myopic model that ignores deeper nodes best explains move choices [6](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- The day’s planning evidence points in the same direction as the bias work: more visible deliberation does not guarantee deeper or better internal search [2](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md) [6](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Oversight, monitoring, and safer action selection
- Behavior Cue Reasoning trains models to emit special cues before implicit or explicit behaviors so external monitors can react to a compressed signal, and the abstract claims this can prune up to 50% of wasted reasoning tokens on math problems [9](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- The same work says the cue mechanism can recover safe actions from 80% of traces that would otherwise end in an unsafe proposal and lift success from 46% to 96% in one setting [9](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- Adaptive auditing of AI systems with anytime-valid guarantees frames audits as dueling null hypotheses and uses Safe Anytime-Valid Inference to support testing by betting, with the abstract claiming valid type-I control and some conclusions from as few as 20 observations [8](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- Anthropic’s NLA toy auditing result also fits this oversight theme, because translating internal states into text improved the auditor’s ability to uncover hidden motivations [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).

## Multi-agent organization, coalition detection, and deployment-time learning
- GraphDC proposes a divide-and-conquer multi-agent system for graph reasoning by splitting a graph into subgraphs, assigning specialized agents to local reasoning, and using a master agent to merge results, with reported gains especially on larger graphs [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- Hidden Coalitions in Multi-Agent AI proposes detecting coalition structure from hidden states instead of behavior alone, using a pairwise mutual-information graph and spectral partitioning to recover programmed or prompt-implied coalitions while avoiding behavior-only false positives [3](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- CASCADE treats deployment-time learning as a third LLM lifecycle stage, combining episodic memory with a contextual-bandit formulation for experience reuse and reporting a 20.9% macro-averaged success-rate gain over zero-shot prompting across 16 tasks [4](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- The same deployment paper is notable because its task suite spans medical diagnosis, legal analysis, code generation, web search, tool use, and embodied interaction, so the gains are presented across a broad operational mix rather than a single benchmark family [4](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Security, structure, and auditability for agentic systems
- Towards Security-Auditable LLM Agents introduces Agent-BOM, a hierarchical attributed directed graph that separates static capability bases from runtime semantic states for security auditing [5](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- The paper claims the representation can reconstruct stealthy attack chains including memory poisoning, tool misuse, supply-chain hijacking, and privilege or trust abuse in agentic attack scenarios [5](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- This and the coalition-detection paper both reflect a broader move toward making agent systems structurally legible enough to analyze, not just observe from outputs alone [3](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md) [5](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Evaluation frameworks and benchmarking
- An Interpretable and Scalable Framework for Evaluating Large Language Models recasts evaluation with Item Response Theory, using majorization-minimization and constrained matrix factorization subproblems [10](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- The abstract emphasizes identifiability and convergence guarantees plus orders-of-magnitude speedups, while reporting comparable or better estimation accuracy on MATH-500 and six Open LLM Leaderboard benchmarks [10](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Test-time scaling and self-improvement
- LLMs Improving LLMs: Agentic Discovery for Test-Time Scaling reframes test-time scaling as environment design, with AutoTTS searching for better TTS strategies automatically rather than relying on hand-crafted heuristics [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- The paper’s core bet is that scaling at inference can be turned into a discovery problem, which aligns with the day’s broader theme of making reasoning and adaptation more measurable and more optimizable [1](../../home/entepi/projects/orbit/data/daily-digest/2026-05-11/ai-research-agent/blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
