# Listwise Policy Optimization: Group-based RLVR as Target-Projection on the LLM Response Simplex

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06139
- Title: Listwise Policy Optimization: Group-based RLVR as Target-Projection on the LLM Response Simplex

## Evidence

> This work reveals that these optimization strategies share a common geometric structure: each implicitly defines a target distribution on the response simplex and projects toward it via first-order approximation.
>
> Building on this insight, we propose Listwise Policy Optimization (LPO) to explicitly conduct the target-projection, which demystifies the implicit target by restricting the proximal RL objective to the response simplex, and then projects the policy via exact divergence minimization.
>
> Across diverse reasoning tasks and LLM backbones, LPO consistently improves training performance over typical policy gradient baselines under matched targets, while intrinsically preserving optimization stability and response diversity.
