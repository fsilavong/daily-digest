# Anisotropic Modality Align

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07825
- Title: Anisotropic Modality Align

## Evidence

> We find that modality representations already share compatible dominant semantic geometry. What truly hinders modality interchangeability is not a simple global shift, but an anisotropic residual structure concentrated along a small number of dominant directions.
>
> Based on this finding, we further propose the principle of anisotropic modality gap alignment: effective modality alignment should align with the target-modality distribution while preserving the semantic structure of the source modality.
>
> Experiments confirm its benefits in both geometric diagnostics and text-only MLLM training.
