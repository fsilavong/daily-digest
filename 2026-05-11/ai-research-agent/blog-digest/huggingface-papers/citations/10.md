# UniSD: Towards a Unified Self-Distillation Framework for Large Language Models

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06597
- Title: UniSD: Towards a Unified Self-Distillation Framework for Large Language Models

## Evidence

> In this paper, we propose UniSD, a unified framework to systematically study self-distillation.
>
> UniSD integrates complementary mechanisms that address supervision reliability, representation alignment, and training stability, including multi-teacher agreement, EMA teacher stabilization, token-level contrastive learning, feature matching, and divergence clipping.
>
> Guided by these insights, we construct UniSDfull ... and achieves the strongest overall performance, improving over the base model by +5.4 points and the strongest baseline by +2.8 points.
