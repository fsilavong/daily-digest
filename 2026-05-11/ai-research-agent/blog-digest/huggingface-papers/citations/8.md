# MISA: Mixture of Indexer Sparse Attention for Long-Context LLM Inference

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07363
- Title: MISA: Mixture of Indexer Sparse Attention for Long-Context LLM Inference

## Evidence

> We propose MISA (Mixture of Indexer Sparse Attention), a drop-in replacement for the DSA indexer that treats its indexer heads as a pool of mixture-of-experts.
>
> With only eight active heads and no additional training, MISA matches the dense DSA indexer on LongBench across DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5 ... It also preserves fully green Needle-in-a-Haystack heatmaps up to a 128K-token context and recovers more than 92% of the tokens selected by the DSA indexer per layer.
>
> Our TileLang kernel delivers roughly a 3.82 times speedup over DSA's original indexer kernel on a single NVIDIA H200 GPU.
