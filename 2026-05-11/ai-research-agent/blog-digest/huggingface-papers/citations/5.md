# 4DThinker: Thinking with 4D Imagery for Dynamic Spatial Understanding

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05997
- Title: 4DThinker: Thinking with 4D Imagery for Dynamic Spatial Understanding

## Evidence

> We present 4DThinker, the first framework that enables VLMs to "think with 4D" through dynamic latent mental imagery, i.e., internally simulating how scenes evolve within the continuous hidden space.
>
> Specifically, we first introduce a scalable, annotation-free data generation pipeline that synthesizes 4D reasoning data from raw videos.
>
> Extensive experiments across multiple dynamic spatial reasoning benchmarks demonstrate that 4DThinker consistently outperforms strong baselines.
