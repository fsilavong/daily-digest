# STARFlow2: Bridging Language Models and Normalizing Flows for Unified Multimodal Generation

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08029
- Title: STARFlow2: Bridging Language Models and Normalizing Flows for Unified Multimodal Generation

## Evidence

> We observe that autoregressive normalizing flows are autoregressive Transformers--sharing the same causal mask, KV-cache mechanism, and left-to-right structure as LLMs--making them the most natural paradigm for true unified multimodal generation.
>
> STARFlow2 ... vertically interleaves a pretrained VLM stream with a TarFlow stream via residual skip connections, both operating under the same causal mask.
>
> Experiments demonstrate strong performance across image generation and multimodal understanding benchmarks.
