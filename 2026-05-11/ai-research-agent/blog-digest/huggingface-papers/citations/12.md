# What Matters for Diffusion-Friendly Latent Manifold? Prior-Aligned Autoencoders for Latent Diffusion

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07915
- Title: What Matters for Diffusion-Friendly Latent Manifold? Prior-Aligned Autoencoders for Latent Diffusion

## Evidence

> We identify three key properties of a diffusion-friendly latent manifold: coherent spatial structure, local manifold continuity, and global manifold semantics.
>
> We find that these properties are more consistent with downstream generation quality than reconstruction fidelity.
>
> On ImageNet 256x256, PAE improves both training efficiency and generation quality over existing tokenizers, reaching performance comparable to RAE with up to 13x faster convergence ... and achieving a new state-of-the-art gFID of 1.03.
