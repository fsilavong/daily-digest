## Safety, alignment, and governance
- Anthropic’s model spec midtraining (MSM) adds a synthetic midtraining phase between pretraining and alignment fine-tuning so models learn the Model Spec’s “what and why” before they are asked to follow it; the key claim is that this can change how later alignment generalizes and substantially reduce agentic misalignment. [Anthropic alignment summary](./blog-digest/anthropic-alignment/content.md)
- A related safety paper argues that emergent misalignment can be explained by feature superposition geometry, where pushing on one feature can strengthen nearby harmful features; it reports a geometry-aware filtering method that reduces misalignment by 34.5% across several LLMs. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)
- A machine-checked Rocq formalization of governed AI workflows claims governance and computational expressivity are orthogonal, with results covering governed Turing completeness, oracle expressivity, decidability boundaries, and semantic transparency on permitted runs. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)
- Multi-agent safety work also shows a training-free defense for infectious jailbreaks in multimodal multi-agent systems, using foresight-guided localized purification to cut maximum cumulative infection from over 95% to below 5.47% while keeping retrieval and semantic metrics close to benign baselines. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs.AI/content.md)

## Agents, orchestration, and reasoning control
- NVIDIA frames agentic systems as structurally different from chat because they call tools, spawn sub-agents, retain memory, and manage context, which makes prompt caching and long-context infrastructure central economic concerns. [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md)
- The same NVIDIA post argues that multi-agent workloads can use up to 15x more tokens than standard chat, and that a long Claude Code session grew from 15K to 156K context tokens before compaction. [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md)
- HeavySkill argues that “heavy thinking” is an internal model skill rather than just an orchestration-time trick, with a two-stage pattern of parallel reasoning followed by summarization that can be scaled with reinforcement learning. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- A survey-and-taxonomy paper on orchestration traces defines spawns, delegation, communication, tool use, return, aggregation, and stopping as the core events to optimize in multi-agent RL, and notes that explicit counterfactual credit and stop-specific RL methods remain sparse. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- A hydrodynamics prototype uses a Layer Execution Graph to build query-specific multi-agent topologies with specialist, consolidator, and reporter roles, and reports 93.6% factual precision across 37 queries. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)
- In GUI-agent research, Faithful-Agent combines faithfulness-oriented SFT with guided advantage estimation to avoid advantage collapse in sparse-reward settings, while another agent paper reports that turn-level truncated on-policy distillation preserves multi-turn tool use and beats several baselines. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md); [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)

## Robotics, embodied systems, and physical-world deployment
- Ai2’s MolmoAct 2 is presented as an open robotics foundation model that outperforms proprietary systems on benchmarks, works out of the box on several real-world tasks, and runs up to 37x faster than MolmoAct. [AllenAI Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md)
- The model combines a Molmo 2-ER embodied-reasoning backbone, an action expert using flow matching, and a KV-cache bridge, and Ai2 also released an open MolmoAct 2-FAST tokenizer plus a 700+ hour open bimanual manipulation dataset. [AllenAI Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md)
- NVIDIA similarly argues that in-vehicle assistants are moving from command-response systems to agentic multimodal copilots, with deployment paths for existing IVI systems, DRIVE AGX Thor, and central car computers under strict latency and privacy constraints. [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md)
- The broader embodied benchmark trend continues with ESARBench for UAV search and rescue, EO-Gym for earth-observation agents, iWorld-Bench for interactive world models, and Workspace-Bench for file-dependent workspace tasks, all stressing that current agents still struggle outside narrow settings. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs.AI/content.md); [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)

## Search, retrieval, and multimodal perception
- OpenSeeker-v2 claims a surprisingly strong pure-SFT recipe for frontier search agents, using only 10.6K trajectories but reaching state-of-the-art results on four search benchmarks at the 30B ReAct scale. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- PRISM inserts a black-box on-policy distillation stage between SFT and RLVR for multimodal RL, using an MoE discriminator to separate perception and reasoning and improving Qwen3-VL accuracy by 4.4 to 6.0 points. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- X2SAM extends any-segmentation from images to videos with a Mask Memory module for temporally consistent video masks, while SplAttN replaces hard projection with differentiable Gaussian splatting to improve point cloud completion from visual cues. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- SVGS improves Gaussian Splatting by giving primitives spatially varying colors and opacity, with movable kernels reported as the best variant for novel-view synthesis on multiple datasets. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- EO-Gym backs Earth Observation agents with 660K+ multimodal files and 35 specialized tools, and the reference EO-Gym-4B baseline improves Pass@3 from 0.49 to 0.74. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)
- Workspace-Bench 1.0 targets workspace learning with 20,476 files and 7,399 rubrics, and its best agent setup still trails humans at 68.7% versus 80% on the benchmark. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)

## Clinical, educational, legal, and professional workflows
- Healthcare AI GYM is a gymnasium-compatible environment for medical agents that spans 10 clinical domains, 3.6K+ tasks, 135 tools, and 828K metrics, aiming to standardize evaluation of medical workflows. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- SymptomAI runs through the Fitbit app as a conversational symptom-assessment agent, and in a randomized study with 13,917 participants it produced more accurate differential diagnoses than independent clinicians given the same dialogue. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- ClinicBot focuses on guideline-grounded clinical chat, prioritizing structured evidence extraction and verifiable citations over generic RAG answers, with a diabetes-oriented demonstration built around ADA guidance. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)
- PERSA trains LLMs to match a professor’s feedback style while preserving correctness, using supervised demonstrations, reward modeling, and PPO, and reports a 96.2% style-alignment score on APPS. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-AI/content.md)
- PatRe turns patent examination into a full-stage Office Action and rebuttal benchmark, capturing both examiner-side analysis and applicant-side response across 480 real-world cases. [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- GR-Ben broadens process-reward evaluation beyond math into science and logic, and finds current PRMs and LLMs are weaker on non-math reasoning, especially knowledge-based and computational errors respectively. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)

[Amazon Science blog digest](./blog-digest/amazon-science-blog/content.md)

## Synthesis
- The day’s strongest pattern is a shift from single-model capability claims toward systems that manage alignment, orchestration, and deployment under real-world constraints, whether in robots, vehicles, clinical apps, or warehouse-like workspaces. [Anthropic alignment summary](./blog-digest/anthropic-alignment/content.md); [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md); [AllenAI Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md)
- Another clear throughline is that evaluation is moving closer to the actual workflow: research teams are building benchmarks around tool use, evidence traces, spatial reasoning, and full multi-turn procedures instead of static Q&A. [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md); [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md)
- Across safety and performance work, several papers and posts converge on the same idea: better generalization often comes from shaping the training process earlier, whether via MSM, pre-alignment distillation, geometry-aware filtering, or skill internalization. [Anthropic alignment summary](./blog-digest/anthropic-alignment/content.md); [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md); [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md)
