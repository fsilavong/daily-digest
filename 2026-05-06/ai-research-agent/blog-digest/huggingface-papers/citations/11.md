# The TTS-STT Flywheel: Synthetic Entity-Dense Audio Closes the Indic ASR Gap Where Commercial and Open-Source Systems Fail

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03073
- Title: The TTS-STT Flywheel: Synthetic Entity-Dense Audio Closes the Indic ASR Gap Where Commercial and Open-Source Systems Fail

## Evidence

> On a synthesised entity-dense Telugu test set (held-out by synthesis system), vasista22/whisper-telugu-large-v2 (open SOTA) achieves Entity-Hit-Rate (EHR) 0.027 and Deepgram Nova-3 (commercial) 0.16.
>
> We close this gap with a self-contained TTS<->STT flywheel: an open-source Indic TTS pipeline synthesises ~22,000 entity-dense Indic-English code-mix utterances at <$50 marginal cost, and a LoRA fine-tune on top of vasista22 achieves EHR 0.473 on the held-out test (17x over open SOTA, 3x over commercial), with read-prose regression bounded to +6.6 pp WER on FLEURS-Te.
>
> All three beta models fall below pre-registered EHR targets (0.75 for Te, 0.65 for Hi/Ta); we report honestly.
>
> A native-human-recorded sanity check (n=20 Telugu) confirms transfer to real speech (beta-Te EHR 0.516 on native vs 0.473 on synth).
