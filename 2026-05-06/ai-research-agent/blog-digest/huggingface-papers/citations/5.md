# SVGS: Enhancing Gaussian Splatting Using Primitives with Spatially Varying Colors

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18966
- Title: SVGS: Enhancing Gaussian Splatting Using Primitives with Spatially Varying Colors

## Evidence

> In this paper, we introduce a new method called SVGS (Spatially Varying Gaussian Splatting) that utilizes spatially varying colors and opacity in a single Gaussian primitive to improve its representation ability.
>
> We have implemented bilinear interpolation, movable kernels, and tiny neural networks as spatially varying functions.
>
> Quantitative and qualitative experimental results demonstrate that all three functions outperform the baseline, with the best movable kernels achieving superior novel view synthesis performance on multiple datasets.
