# PatRe: A Full-Stage Office Action and Rebuttal Generation Benchmark for Patent Examination

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03571
- Title: PatRe: A Full-Stage Office Action and Rebuttal Generation Benchmark for Patent Examination

## Evidence

> We introduce PatRe, the first benchmark that models the full patent examination lifecycle, including Office Action generation and applicant rebuttal.
>
> PatRe comprises 480 real-world cases and supports both oracle and retrieval-simulated evaluation settings.
>
> Extensive experiments across various LLMs reveal critical insights into model performance, including differences between proprietary and open-source models, as well as task asymmetries between examiner analysis and applicant-side rebuttal.
