# Healthcare AI GYM for Medical Agents

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02943
- Title: Healthcare AI GYM for Medical Agents

## Evidence

> We present a comprehensive empirical study of multi-turn agentic RL for medical AI, built on a gymnasium-compatible environment spanning 10 clinical domains with 3.6K+ tasks, 135 domain-specific tools, and a knowledge base of 828K medical passages.
>
> Our analysis reveals that agentic multi-turn structure degrades into verbose single-turn monologues, characterized by monotonic length explosion and a simultaneous erosion of tool-use frequency.
>
> We find that vanilla GRPO achieves strong final accuracy on some benchmarks but suffers from training instability, evidenced by significant oscillations in response length and prolonged convergence periods.
>
> TT-OPD achieves the best performance on 10 of 18 benchmarks with an average +3.9~pp improvement over the non-RL baseline with faster early convergence, controlled response length, and sustained multi-turn tool use.
