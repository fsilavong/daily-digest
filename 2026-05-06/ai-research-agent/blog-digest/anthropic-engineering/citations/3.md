# Harness design for long-running application development

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps
- Title: Harness design for long-running application development

## Evidence

> A generator/evaluator multi-agent harness can improve both subjective frontend design and long-running full-stack coding.

> Uses a GAN-inspired loop with a generator and evaluator; for coding, the system adds planner/generator/evaluator roles, context resets or compaction/handoffs, and structured artifacts between sessions.

> The evaluator-guided loop produced more distinctive frontend designs and sustained richer full-stack application development over multi-hour autonomous sessions.

> Self-evaluation is unreliable because models tend to praise their own outputs; resets add orchestration complexity, token overhead, and latency, and not every iteration improves linearly.

> Shows that harness design—not just prompting/model choice—can materially affect long-running agent quality and creative output.
