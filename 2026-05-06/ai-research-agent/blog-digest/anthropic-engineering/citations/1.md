# Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents
- Title: Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands

## Evidence

> Managed Agents are designed to decouple the “brain” (Claude + harness), “hands” (tools/sandboxes), and “session” (durable event log) so each can evolve independently.

> Replaces a single coupled container with interfaces like `execute()`, `wake(sessionId)`, `getSession(id)`, and `emitEvent(id, event)`; the harness becomes stateless/cattle-like while sessions live externally.

> Anthropic reports TTFT improved materially after decoupling, with p50 down ~60% and p95 down >90%.

> Earlier assumptions in harnesses can go stale as models improve; coupled designs created debugging, recovery, and VPC-connection problems.

> Enables long-horizon agent infrastructure that is more resilient, more secure (credentials kept out of sandbox), and easier to adapt as models and deployments change.
