# Quantifying infrastructure noise in agentic coding evals

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/infrastructure-noise
- Title: Quantifying infrastructure noise in agentic coding evals

## Evidence

> Agentic coding benchmark scores are confounded by infrastructure configuration, so small leaderboard differences can reflect runtime setup rather than model capability.

> Anthropic varied resource headroom across Terminal-Bench 2.0 and SWE-bench runs while holding model, harness, and task set constant, measuring both infra errors and success rates.

> On Terminal-Bench 2.0, the gap between most- and least-resourced setups was 6 points; infra errors fell from 5.8% to 0.5% as headroom increased, and SWE-bench also improved modestly with more RAM.

> Up to ~3x headroom mostly reduces spurious failures, but beyond that extra resources begin changing what the eval measures; time-of-day, cluster health, and egress bandwidth may also matter.

> Benchmark consumers should treat small score deltas skeptically unless resource specs and enforcement methodology are documented and matched.
