# MolmoAct 2 additional excerpt note
- Source: https://allenai.org/blog/molmoact2
- Title: MolmoAct 2: An open foundation for robots that work in the real world

## Evidence

> Some of the in-lab evaluations described here were conducted on YAM arms donated by I2RT Robotics. I2RT had no role in the development of MolmoAct 2, the design of the evaluations, or the reporting of these results.

> To validate MolmoAct 2 beyond our own lab evaluations, we retained Cortex AI, a robotics data and evaluation company, to conduct a third-party benchmark of MolmoAct 2’s real-world fine-tuning performance.

> MolmoAct 2 achieved the highest average score at 0.51, ahead of OpenVLA-OFT at 0.36, π0.5 at 0.32, Cosmos Policy at 0.16, and X-VLA at 0.05.
