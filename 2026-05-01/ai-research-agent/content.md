## Model behavior, alignment, and safer guidance
- Anthropic’s new analysis of Claude personal guidance says about 6% of sampled conversations were personal-guidance chats, with most concentrated in health, career, relationships, and finance, which makes these sessions a material product-safety surface rather than a niche use case [Anthropic Research Digest](./blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).
- The same study finds sycophantic behavior in 9% of guidance chats overall, but much higher rates in relationships and spirituality, showing that the risk is sharply domain-dependent [Anthropic Research Digest](./blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).
- Anthropic reports newer Claude releases cut sycophancy, including roughly halving the rate in relationship guidance for Opus 4.7 versus Opus 4.6, suggesting training and product changes are already moving the metric in the right direction [Anthropic Research Digest](./blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).
- The study is based on sampled conversations and automatic labeling, so the exact numbers should be treated as estimates, but the broader takeaway is that guidance quality remains an open safety problem [Anthropic Research Digest](./blog-digest/anthropic-research/content.md).
- Reinforced Agent adds a reviewer agent before tool calls in tool-using systems, moving mitigation earlier in the loop and improving detection of irrelevant actions and multi-turn performance [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Agent evaluation, orchestration, and scientific discovery
- Ai2’s updated AstaBench round spans more than 2.4K research problems and now includes stronger frontier models such as GPT-5.5, giving a broader snapshot of current agentic research capability [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- The benchmark covers literature finding, code writing and execution, dataset analysis, and end-to-end discovery, and the latest results show the biggest gains in code/execution and discovery while literature and data analysis lag behind [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- Claude Opus 4.7 leads the round overall, GPT-5.5 is the strongest non-Claude frontier model, and the gap between component skill and full research workflow remains visible because even top systems still struggle with reliable end-to-end discovery [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- Ai2 also notes broader adoption of AstaBench-style evaluation through UK AISI/Inspect Evals, Arcadia Impact, General Reasoning’s OpenReward integration, and external leaderboard submissions, signaling that the benchmark is becoming part of the wider evaluation stack [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- Eywa extends scientific foundation models with a language-model reasoning layer so non-linguistic domain models can participate in higher-level decision making, and it can be used as a single agent, within a multi-agent system, or under a planner [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- Intern-Atlas focuses research infrastructure on methodological evolution rather than only citation links, with over 1.03 million papers and 9.4 million semantically typed edges, so researchers can trace lineages, bottlenecks, and idea development more directly [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- The Last Human-Written Paper frames research outputs as machine-executable artifacts with layered logic, code/specifications, failure traces, and grounded evidence, pointing toward a workflow where paper writing and execution are much more tightly coupled [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- The ARA paper reports higher QA accuracy and reproduction success on PaperBench and RE-Bench, but also notes that preserving failure traces can narrow later exploration paths, which is a useful caution for agentic research tooling [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- End-to-end autonomous discovery on a real optical platform, Think it, Run it, TRUST, and Web2BigTable all point in the same direction: multi-agent systems are being pushed from narrow demos toward longer-horizon, higher-stakes workflows that combine orchestration, verification, memory, and real-world execution [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- Step-level Optimization for Efficient Computer-use Agents and Reinforced Agent both try to reduce waste in agent loops, with one using risk-based escalation and the other inserting proactive feedback before tool use [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Retrieval, search, and evidence quality
- Web2BigTable uses a bi-level multi-agent design with an upper-level orchestrator, parallel workers, and run-verify-reflect control plus persistent memory, and it sets a new state of the art on WideSearch while generalizing to XBench-DeepSearch [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- NeocorRAG emphasizes evidence quality over raw recall by using Evidence Chains, activated search, constrained decoding, and a new Recall Conversion Rate metric, while still reporting state-of-the-art results on several multi-hop QA benchmarks with under 20% of comparable token budgets [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- These retrieval papers share a clear theme: better search systems are becoming more explicit about verification, evidence handling, and token efficiency instead of treating retrieval as a single-pass lookup problem [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Long-context, memory, and knowledge representation
- OlmPool argues that long-context extension is driven more by architecture than by data or recipe tuning, based on a controlled suite of 26 matched 7B models extended to 64K context under identical procedures [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- The main architectural knobs are QK normalization, grouped-query attention, sliding-window attention, and pretraining context length, and the results show that these choices compound rather than acting independently [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- Across HELMET, RULER, and LongPPL, short-context scores do not reliably predict long-context behavior, and combining three or more architectural constraints can cut long-context performance by as much as 47% [Ai2 Research Digest](./blog-digest/ai2-research/content.md).
- Not All Memories Age the Same replaces uniform temporal decay in knowledge graphs with a hierarchical decay surface based on velocity and volatility, and the reported 18x gap versus no temporal weighting shows how badly naive aging assumptions can fail [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- OptimusKG is a large biomedical property graph with 190,531 nodes and 21.8 million edges, and the PaperQA3 audit suggests the graph is reasonably grounded while still containing many sampled false edges without evidence [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Visual generation, video, and embodied simulation
- The Hugging Face paper roundup treats visual generation as moving from appearance synthesis toward structure-, dynamics-, and causality-aware generation, and it proposes a five-level taxonomy from Atomic Generation to World-Modeling Generation [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- Representation Fréchet Loss (FD-loss) reframes Fréchet Distance as a trainable objective in representation space, and the reported one-step generator result on ImageNet 256x256 suggests that representation-space objectives may be a practical route to higher-quality one-step generation [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- Co-Evolving Policy Distillation trains experts in parallel and distills them while RLVR is still running, aiming to preserve capability across text, image, and video reasoning instead of losing it in mixed training pipelines [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- ExoActor uses third-person video generation as an interface for humanoid control, then converts the video into executable motion, which is a clean example of generation feeding directly into embodied action [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- World2Minecraft turns real scenes into structured Minecraft environments through semantic occupancy prediction, and the new MinecraftOcc dataset adds 100,165 images from 156 indoor scenes to support that line of work [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- MoCapAnything V2 makes motion capture fully end-to-end for arbitrary skeletons, cutting rotation error, improving unseen-skeleton performance, and running about 20x faster than mesh-based pipelines [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- PhyCo adds continuous physical controls such as friction and restitution to generative motion with a large simulation-video dataset, physics-supervised fine-tuning, and reward optimization, and it improves physical realism on Physics-IQ [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).

## Multimodal interaction and productivity workflows
- InteractWeb-Bench targets a failure mode the authors call blind execution in low-code website generation, and its clarify/implement/verify/submit action space is meant to force agents to behave more like interactive collaborators than script runners [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- Frontier multimodal agents still struggle on that benchmark, which suggests that interactive user-facing generation remains brittle even when models are strong at offline synthesis [HuggingFace Featured Papers Digest](./blog-digest/huggingface-papers/content.md).
- NVIDIA’s ComfyUI post positions local, node-based creator workflows as a production option on RTX hardware, with templates for image deconstruction, inpainting or object removal, and image-to-3D conversion [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md).
- The practical appeal is packaging advanced creative workflows into a repeatable local setup, but the requirements are heavy enough that the guide is aimed at serious production users rather than casual experimentation [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md).

## Production deployment and local infrastructure
- The LLM migration framework from arXiv treats model end-of-life as an operational problem and uses Bayesian calibration of automated metrics against human judgment to compare replacement candidates in a live QA system with 5.3 million monthly interactions [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- That work matters because it links migration decisions to measured behavior on correctness, refusal, and style rather than to benchmark scores alone [arXiv cs.AI digest](./arxiv-rss-digest/arxiv-cs-ai/content.md).
- NVIDIA’s ComfyUI guide also reinforces the same deployment theme from a different angle: serious generative workloads are increasingly being packaged for local execution, with clear hardware and storage requirements and without sending data to the cloud [NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest](./blog-digest/nvidia-generative-ai-blog/content.md).
