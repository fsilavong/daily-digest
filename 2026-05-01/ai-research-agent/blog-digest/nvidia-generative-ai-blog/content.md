# NVIDIA Generative AI Blog Digest

## How to Build, Run, and Scale High-Quality Creator Workflows in ComfyUI
Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-build-run-and-scale-high-quality-creator-workflows-in-comfyui/

- NVIDIA frames ComfyUI as a local, node-based way for creative teams to run production-ready generative AI workflows on RTX hardware without cloud dependency or data leaving the machine. [1](./citations/1.md)
- The post centers three pre-built workflow templates from the NVIDIA GenAI Creator Toolkit: image deconstruction into layers, targeted inpainting/object removal, and image-to-3D conversion. [1](./citations/1.md)
- The workflows are presented as load-a-template, upload-an-image, and click-Run experiences, with outputs described as layered PNGs, cleaned-up edited images, or a GLB 3D model depending on the template. [1](./citations/1.md)
- The setup requirements are nontrivial: the guide calls for RTX GPUs with 24–32 GB VRAM minimum depending on OS, 150 GB disk space, and the 3D workflow is Windows-only and requires Hugging Face access approval. [1](./citations/1.md)
- The post matters because it packages advanced creative pipelines into a repeatable local workflow for design, visualization, compositing, and previs teams. [1](./citations/1.md)
