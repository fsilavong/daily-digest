# How to Build, Run, and Scale High-Quality Creator Workflows in ComfyUI

- Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-build-run-and-scale-high-quality-creator-workflows-in-comfyui/
- Title: How to Build, Run, and Scale High-Quality Creator Workflows in ComfyUI

## Evidence

> NVIDIA frames ComfyUI as a local, node-based way for creative teams to run production-ready generative AI workflows on RTX hardware without cloud dependency or data leaving the machine.
>
> The post walks through three pre-built ComfyUI template workflows from the NVIDIA GenAI Creator Toolkit: image deconstruction into layers, targeted inpainting/object removal, and image-to-3D conversion.
>
> These workflows are intended to be runnable directly in ComfyUI by loading a template, uploading an image, and clicking Run; the post says they produce layered PNGs, cleaned-up edited images, or a GLB 3D model, depending on the workflow.
>
> Hardware and model requirements are substantial: the guide calls for RTX GPUs with 24–32 GB VRAM minimum depending on OS, 150 GB disk space, and the 3D workflow is Windows-only and requires Hugging Face access approval.
>
> It lowers the barrier for non-expert teams to use advanced genAI creative pipelines locally, with a repeatable setup suitable for design, visualization, compositing, and previs work.
