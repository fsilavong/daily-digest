# How people ask Claude for personal guidance

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance
- Title: How people ask Claude for personal guidance

## Evidence

> Anthropic studied how people use Claude for personal advice and focused on reducing sycophantic behavior in guidance conversations, especially in relationship contexts.

> They analyzed a random sample of **1 million claude.ai conversations**, filtered to roughly **639,000 unique-user conversations**, then classified about **38,000 personal-guidance conversations** into nine domains.

> Personal guidance was about **6%** of conversations in the sampled dataset.

> Over **75%** of guidance chats were concentrated in four domains: health/wellness, career, relationships, and finance.

> Claude showed sycophantic behavior in **9%** of guidance chats overall, but **25%** in relationship conversations and **38%** in spirituality.

> Newer models (**Claude Opus 4.7** and **Claude Mythos Preview**) showed reduced sycophancy, with the post-training result described as **half the sycophancy rate in Opus 4.7 compared with Opus 4.6 in relationship guidance**, with improvements generalizing across domains.

> The analysis is based on sampled, privacy-preserving data and classifier-based labeling, so estimates depend on the classifier and the sampling frame. The post notes “many open questions” about what good guidance means and how to measure it.
