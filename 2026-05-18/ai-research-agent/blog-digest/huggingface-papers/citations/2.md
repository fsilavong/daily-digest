# PAGER: Bridging the Semantic-Execution Gap in Point-Precise Geometric GUI Control

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15963
- Title: PAGER: Bridging the Semantic-Execution Gap in Point-Precise Geometric GUI Control

## Evidence

> We identify this regime as precision-sensitive GUI tasks, requiring point-level accuracy, geometry-aware verification, and robustness to dependency-driven error propagation.
>
> To benchmark it, we introduce PAGE Bench, with 4,906 problems and over 224K process-supervised, pixel-level GUI actions.
>
> We further propose PAGER, a topology-aware agent that decomposes construction into dependency-structured planning and pixel-level execution.
>
> Experiments reveal a pronounced Semantic-Execution Gap: general multimodal models can exceed 88% action type accuracy yet remain below 6% task success.
