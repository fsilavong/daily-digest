# Solvita: Enhancing Large Language Models for Competitive Programming via Agentic Evolution

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15301
- Title: Solvita: Enhancing Large Language Models for Competitive Programming via Agentic Evolution

## Evidence

> We present Solvita, an agentic evolution framework that enables continuous learning without requiring weight updates to the underlying LLM.
>
> Solvita reorganizes problem-solving into a closed-loop system of strategy selection, program synthesis, certified supervision, and targeted hacking, executed by four specialized agents: Planner, Solver, Oracle, and Hacker.
>
> Evaluated across CodeContests, APPS, AetherCode, and live Codeforces rounds, Solvita establishes a new state-of-the-art among code-generation agents, outperforming existing multi-agent pipelines and nearly doubling the accuracy of single-pass baselines.
