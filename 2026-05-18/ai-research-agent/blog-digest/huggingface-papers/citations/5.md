# Learning from Failures: Correction-Oriented Policy Optimization with Verifiable Rewards

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14539
- Title: Learning from Failures: Correction-Oriented Policy Optimization with Verifiable Rewards

## Evidence

> RLVR training is often hindered by sparse binary rewards and weak credit assignment, resulting in ambiguous optimization signals and underutilization of the useful information embedded in failed trajectories.
>
> We propose Correction-Oriented Policy Optimization (CIPO), a simple and effective extension to RLVR that converts on-policy failed trajectories into correction-oriented supervision, without relying on any external signals.
>
> Extensive experiments across 11 benchmarks spanning mathematical reasoning and code generation demonstrate that CIPO consistently and significantly outperforms strong baselines in both reasoning and correction performance.
