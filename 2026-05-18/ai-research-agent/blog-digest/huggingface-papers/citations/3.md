# ReactiveGWM: Steering NPC in Reactive Game World Models

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15256
- Title: ReactiveGWM: Steering NPC in Reactive Game World Models

## Evidence

> Current game world models simulate environments from a subjective, player-centric perspective. However, by treating the Non-Player Character (NPC) merely as background pixels, these models cannot capture interactions between the player and NPC.
>
> We introduce ReactiveGWM, a reactive game world model that synthesizes dynamic interactions between the player and NPC.
>
> Player actions are injected into the diffusion backbone via a lightweight additive bias, while high-level NPC responses (e.g., Offense, Control, Defense) are grounded through cross-attention modules.
>
> Evaluated on two Street Fighter games, ReactiveGWM maintains fine-grain player controllability while achieving robust, prompt-aligned NPC strategy adherence.
