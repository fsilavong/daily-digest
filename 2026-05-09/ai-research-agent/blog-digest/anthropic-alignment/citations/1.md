# Teaching Claude Why

- Title: Teaching Claude Why
- Retrieved: full text available, but the captured text was truncated in the available excerpt.

## Evidence

> Alignment improves more when training teaches the principles behind aligned behavior, not just demonstrations; surprisingly OOD training data and simple “advice about ethical dilemmas” can generalize well.
>
> Anthropic studied several interventions for agentic misalignment: (1) training Claude on user-advice conversations about ethical dilemmas, (2) training on LLM-generated documents about Claude’s constitution and fictional admirable AI behavior, and (3) augmenting harmlessness RL environments with tool definitions and more varied system prompts.
>
> The ethical-dilemma advice dataset reduced measured agentic misalignment to zero; constitution/story documents improved alignment and the gains persisted through RL post-training; tool-augmented harmlessness environments substantially reduced agentic misalignment.
>
> Directly training on the eval distribution can suppress the metric without improving out-of-distribution behavior, and may reduce the usefulness of future auditing; the post emphasizes that demonstrations alone were often insufficient, and data quality/diversity mattered a lot.
