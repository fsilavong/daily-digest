# EMO: Pretraining mixture of experts for emergent modularity

- Source: https://allenai.org/blog/emo
- Title: EMO: Pretraining mixture of experts for emergent modularity

## Evidence

> EMO is a mixture-of-experts (MoE) model pretrained with end-to-end data-driven expert grouping, where the modular structure emerges naturally from the data rather than being imposed manually.

> EMO is a 1B-active, 14B-total-parameter MoE model with 8 active experts out of 128 total experts, trained on 1 trillion tokens.

> Selective expert use can preserve near full-model performance while using only 12.5% of the total experts.

> On general-purpose benchmarks, EMO matches standard MoE performance, and when evaluated with only 25% of experts active it drops by about 1% absolute, and by about 3% at 12.5%.

> Our method leverages document boundaries as weak supervision, encouraging tokens in the same document to share a restricted expert pool.

> We release the full EMO model, a standard MoE baseline, training code, and an interactive visualization.
