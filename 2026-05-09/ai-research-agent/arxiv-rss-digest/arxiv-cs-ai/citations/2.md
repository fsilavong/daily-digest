# Partial Evidence Bench: Benchmarking Authorization-Limited Evidence in Agentic Systems

- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05379
- Title: Partial Evidence Bench: Benchmarking Authorization-Limited Evidence in Agentic Systems
- Published: 2026-05-09

## Evidence

> This paper introduces Partial Evidence Bench, a deterministic benchmark for measuring that failure mode. The benchmark ships three scenario families -- due diligence, compliance audit, and security incident response -- with 72 tasks total, ACL-partitioned corpora, oracle complete answers, oracle authorized-view answers, oracle completeness judgments, and structured gap-report oracles.
>
> It evaluates systems along four surfaces: answer correctness, completeness awareness, gap-report quality, and unsafe completeness behavior.
>
> Checked-in baselines show that silent filtering is catastrophically unsafe across all shipped families, while explicit fail-and-report behavior eliminates unsafe completeness without collapsing the task into trivial abstention.
>
> Preliminary real-model runs show model-dependent and scenario-sensitive differences in whether systems overclaim completeness, conservatively underclaim, or report incompleteness in an enterprise-usable form.
