# Focus areas for The Anthropic Institute

- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-institute-agenda
- Title: Focus areas for The Anthropic Institute

## Evidence

> The Anthropic Institute is presented as an effort to study AI’s real-world impacts from inside a frontier lab and to publish research, data, and tools for public use.
>
> The agenda is organized around four pillars: economic diffusion, threats and resilience, AI systems in the wild, and AI-driven R&D.
>
> Concrete planned work includes expanding and updating the Anthropic Economic Index with more granular, high-frequency labor and usage data, studying resilience and security investment needs, measuring how Anthropic’s own work accelerates via AI tools, and producing early-warning signals and public-facing research.
>
> The post grounds the agenda in observed internal changes such as shifts in labor, emerging threats, and AI speeding up AI R&D.
>
> This is explicitly described as a living agenda rather than a fixed plan, so the questions and priorities are expected to change as evidence accumulates.
