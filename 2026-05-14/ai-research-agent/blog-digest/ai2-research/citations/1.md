# Introducing AIMIP: The AI weather and climate model intercomparison project

- Source: https://allenai.org/blog/aimip
- Title: Introducing AIMIP: The AI weather and climate model intercomparison project

## Evidence

> AIMIP brings together multiple modeling groups ... around a shared benchmark experiment and dataset

> AIMIP Phase 1 ... designed to compare AI climate models under a common setup

> Models must be trained only on the ERA5 historical observations from 1979-2014

> the best AI climate models can reduce the time-averaged error in fields like near-surface air temperature by a factor of 2

> the models’ predictions diverge significantly in this out-of-sample case, with some producing what appear to be physically implausible results

> Generalization ... will be critical if these models are to be widely adopted by the scientific community
