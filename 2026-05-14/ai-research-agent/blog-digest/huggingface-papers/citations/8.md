# Results and Retrospective Analysis of the CODS 2025 AssetOpsBench Challenge
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08518
- Title: Results and Retrospective Analysis of the CODS 2025 AssetOpsBench Challenge

## Evidence

> We combine final rank sheets, a 300-submission server log, 149-team registrations, best-submission exports, the organizer winners report, the companion system paper, and verified planning-track source trees.
>
> The public planning leaderboard saturates at 72.73%, and richer prompts do not improve that peak.
>
> Public and private scores correlate moderately in planning (r=0.69) but negatively in execution (r=-0.13), with several 45.45% public execution systems reaching 63.64% on the hidden set.
>
> Successful execution methods mostly improve guardrails -- response selection, contamination cleanup, fallback, and context control -- rather than novel agent architectures.
