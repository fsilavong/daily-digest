# AnyFlow: Any-Step Video Diffusion Model with On-Policy Flow Map Distillation
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13724
- Title: AnyFlow: Any-Step Video Diffusion Model with On-Policy Flow Map Distillation

## Evidence

> We introduce AnyFlow, the first any-step video diffusion distillation framework based on flow maps.
>
> Instead of distilling a model for only a few fixed sampling steps, AnyFlow optimizes the full ODE sampling trajectory.
>
> Flow Map Backward Simulation decomposes a full Euler rollout into shortcut flow-map transitions, enabling efficient on-policy distillation that reduces test-time errors.
>
> Extensive experiments across both bidirectional and causal architectures, at scales ranging from 1.3B to 14B parameters, demonstrate that AnyFlow achieves performance matches or surpasses consistency-based counterparts in the few-step regime, while scaling with sampling step budgets.
