# Training Long-Context Vision-Language Models Effectively with Generalization Beyond 128K Context
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13831
- Title: Training Long-Context Vision-Language Models Effectively with Generalization Beyond 128K Context

## Evidence

> We present a systematic study of long-context continued pre-training for LVLMs, extending a 7B model from 32K to 128K context with extensive ablations on long-document data.
>
> We first show that long-document VQA is substantially more effective than OCR transcription.
>
> Balanced data outperforms target-length-focused data (e.g., 128K), suggesting that long-context ability requires generalizable key-information retrieval across various lengths and positions.
>
> MMProLong improves long-document VQA scores by 7.1% and maintains strong performance at 256K and 512K contexts beyond its 128K training window, without additional training.
