# Edit-Compass & EditReward-Compass: A Unified Benchmark for Image Editing and Reward Modeling
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13062
- Title: Edit-Compass & EditReward-Compass: A Unified Benchmark for Image Editing and Reward Modeling

## Evidence

> Edit-Compass contains 2,388 carefully annotated instances spanning six progressively challenging task categories, covering capabilities such as world knowledge reasoning, visual reasoning, and multi-image editing.
>
> Edit-Compass adopts a fine-grained multidimensional evaluation framework based on structured reasoning and carefully designed scoring rubrics.
>
> EditReward-Compass contains 2,251 preference pairs that simulate realistic reward modeling scenarios during RL optimization.
