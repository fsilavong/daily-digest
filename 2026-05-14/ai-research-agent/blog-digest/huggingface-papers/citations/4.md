# Qwen-Image-VAE-2.0 Technical Report
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13565
- Title: Qwen-Image-VAE-2.0 Technical Report

## Evidence

> We present Qwen-Image-VAE-2.0, a suite of high-compression Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) that achieve significant advances in both reconstruction fidelity and diffusability.
>
> We adopt an improved architecture featuring Global Skip Connections (GSC) and expanded latent channels.
>
> We scale training to billions of images and incorporate a synthetic rendering engine to improve performance in text-rich scenarios.
>
> Qwen-Image-VAE-2.0 achieves state-of-the-art reconstruction performance, demonstrating exceptional capabilities in both general domains and text-rich scenarios at high compression ratio.
