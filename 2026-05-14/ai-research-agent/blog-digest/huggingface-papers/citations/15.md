# AgentLens: Revealing The Lucky Pass Problem in SWE-Agent Evaluation
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12925
- Title: AgentLens: Revealing The Lucky Pass Problem in SWE-Agent Evaluation

## Evidence

> We evaluate 2,614 OpenHands trajectories from eight model backends on 60 SWE-bench Verified tasks.
>
> Among passing trajectories in this subset, 10.7% exhibit behavior we call a Lucky Pass: regression cycles, blind retries, missing verification, or temporally disordered exploration, implementation, and verification.
>
> We introduce AgentLens, a framework for process-level assessment of SWE-agent trajectories, and release AgentLens-Bench, a dataset of 1,815 trajectories annotated with quality scores, waste signals, divergence points, and 47 task-level Prefix Tree Acceptor (PTA) references.
>
> Lucky rates range from 0.5% to 23.2%, and some models move by as many as five rank positions when ranked by quality score instead of pass rate.
