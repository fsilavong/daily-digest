## Retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge-graph traversal

### PersonalAI 2.0: Enhancing knowledge graph traversal/retrieval with planning mechanism for Personalized LLM Agents
- PersonalAI 2.0 (PAI-2) is presented as a framework for LLM-based systems that integrates external knowledge graphs and a dynamic, multistage query-processing pipeline [1](./citations/1.md).
- The abstract says the design supports adaptive, iterative information search using extracted entities, matched graph vertices, and generated clue-queries [1](./citations/1.md).
- It is evaluated on six benchmarks: Natural Questions, TriviaQA, HotpotQA, 2WikiMultihopQA, MuSiQue, and DiaASQ [1](./citations/1.md).
- Claimed gains include a 4% average improvement by LLM-as-a-Judge across four benchmarks over LightRAG, RAPTOR, and HippoRAG 2, plus an 18% boost from the search-plan enhancement mechanism and a 6% average gain from graph traversal algorithms such as BeamSearch and WaterCircles versus a standard flatten retriever [1](./citations/1.md).
- The abstract also claims SOTA on MINE-1 with an 89% information-retention score using 7–14B LLMs [1](./citations/1.md).
